# subset.txt -- the forms of LLVM text the compiler writes, by name: what scripts/ll-census.cho may find in a module (docs/native-backend.md, section 3).
# A line is `KIND NAME` as the census prints it, without the count; one that ends in `*` accepts every form that begins so; `#` begins a comment.
# tests/commands/native_subset.sh fails, naming the form, when the compiler's own module or the probe for no system holds a form that is not here: a change
# to compiler/emit.cho or to compiler/runtime/ that widens what a reader of this text must take is then a change to this file, in the same pull request,
# and to the table of section 3 of the document.
#
# Drawn on 2026-11-26 from the census of 459 modules: every program of tests/accept and examples that is one file, and the compiler, for linux, darwin and
# linux-aarch64 (`--host`), and the six probes of tests/probes that build for aarch64-none-elf. Section 3 of the document says which forms no measured
# program used and the sources can still write; there were none left out of this list for that reason.
