not fetched, nothing derived from them.

## Derived

`tests/vectors/rsa_sign/nist_siggen15.txt` is derived from the NIST file above by
`scripts/rsa_sign_vectors.py` (p and q recovered from n, e and d; the
signatures are NIST's). `tests/vectors/rsa_sign/{valid,malformed}.txt` are
generated by `scripts/rsa_sign_keys.py` from a fixed seed and use nothing
third-party.
