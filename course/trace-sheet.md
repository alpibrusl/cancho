# Delegation trace

One of these per delegation, in every Part B lesson. Fill it in *while* you
work, not afterwards: a reconstructed record is flattering and useless.

**Lesson / task:**   **Date:**

---

### 1. What I asked for

*The actual words — the brief and the contract — not a summary of them.*

### 2. What "done" would mean

*Written before looking at any result. A contract that does not exist yet
cannot be the answer.*

### 3. One way it could pass all of that and still be wrong

*Also before looking. If you cannot think of one, you have not looked long
enough. There is always one.*

### 4. What came back

*One sentence. The refusal count if it was refused.*

### 5. What I ran

*The commands, and what each said. "I read it and it looked fine" is not a
check; if that is what happened, write that down.*

| `check` | `satisfy` / `test` | `authority` |
|---|---|---|
| | | |

### 6. What I did **not** check

*The most important box. An honest "I did not verify this" is a respectable
thing to hand someone. An unverified claim presented as a verified one is
not.*

### 7. What I caught

*Anything wrong, missing, or decided for me that I did not ask about.*

*Keep every one. After a handful, read them together and look for your own
pattern, the kind of thing you forget every time. Nobody else can tell you
what yours is.*
